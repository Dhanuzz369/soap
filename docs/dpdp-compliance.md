# DPDP Act checklist for GoodBiz Hygiene

A plain checklist of what India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** (the "DPDP Act") asks of a small Data Fiduciary, where the website or the business already meets it, and what the owner must do. GoodBiz Global is the Data Fiduciary for GoodBiz Hygiene (see the Privacy Policy, `/privacy/`).

This is a working checklist, not legal advice and not a certificate. It does not say the site is "compliant": it says what is in place and what is left to do. Have a lawyer review it if the business grows, starts marketing to consumers, or adds analytics, advertising or other tools that collect personal data.

**When it applies.** Commencement is **phased; check the current dates.** The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025, and most obligations on Data Fiduciaries apply about 18 months after the Rules were notified (around May 2027). Confirm the dates on the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) website before relying on them.

**What the site collects** (the full list is in the Privacy Policy): enquiry details (name, company, email and/or phone, products, quantity, destination, shipping terms, own brand, notes, page language and referring page), saved to a Google Sheet when the buyer taps Next on step 1 and as each later step is answered; the alert email through Gmail; Vercel's server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL); and, only for visitors who click Accept in the cookie banner, Google Analytics usage data (pages, product and step keys, contact-link clicks, device, approximate location; never names, contact details or notes). No advertising or social media trackers (see the Cookie Policy, `/cookies/`).

## Checklist

| Obligation (DPDP Act) | Where it is met | What the owner must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Notice and legal basis** (s.5 to s.7) | The Privacy Policy says what is collected, why, and the basis: data given voluntarily for a specified purpose, replying to the enquiry (s.7(a)). Step 1 of the form says the details are saved when the buyer taps Next, only to reply, never sold or added to a mailing list, and links to the policy, in all 12 languages. No consent is collected for anything else. | Use enquiry data only to reply, quote, send samples and follow up on that enquiry. Do not add enquirers to newsletters or campaigns. If that ever changes, a separate consent and notice (s.5, s.6) are needed first, and the policy must be updated. |
| **Contact for questions** (s.8(9)) | The Privacy Policy names hygiene@goodbizglobal.com and +91 88847 99369 for questions about personal data. The same details are in every page's footer and on the About page. | Make sure someone reads that inbox and answers privacy questions. Keep the policy's contact details current. |
| **Rights and the request route** (s.11 to s.14: access to a summary, correction, completion, updating and erasure, grievance redressal, nomination) | The Privacy Policy lists the rights and the route: an email with the subject "Privacy request", identity check where needed. | Handle each request: find the person's rows in the enquiry sheet (and Gmail), send the summary, correct or delete as asked, and record what was done (date, request, action). |
| **Grievance timelines** (s.8(10), s.13; the Rules) | The policy promises a reply as soon as possible and within 30 days, and resolution within the time the law allows, at most 90 days. It tells the person they can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. | **Reply to privacy requests within 30 days.** Calendar a reminder for every request. Check the current Rules for the exact grievance period. |
| **Retention and erasure** (s.8(7)) | The policy says: enquiries that do not lead to business are deleted within 24 months of the last contact; customer data is kept for the business relationship and then as long as Indian tax, customs and foreign trade laws require; hosting logs for a short period (Vercel's retention). The browser storage on the site is cleared when the tab closes or by the visitor (Cookie Policy). | **Calendar a deletion sweep** (for example every quarter) for enquiry rows, alert emails and WhatsApp chats older than 24 months with no business. Delete data on request unless the law requires it to be kept. |
| **Security safeguards** (s.8(5)) | The site is static and served over HTTPS; no database or admin panel on the website; security headers in `vercel.json`. | **Turn on 2-step verification on the Google account** that owns the sheet, the Apps Script and Gmail, and on the Vercel account. **Restrict sheet sharing** to the people who answer enquiries (no "anyone with the link"). Remove access when someone leaves. |
| **Breach notification** (s.8(6)) | The policy says the business will inform the affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. | **Keep a simple breach log** (date, what happened, data affected, people affected, actions, who was told and when). If a breach happens, inform the Board and each affected person in the form and within the time the Rules require (check the current Rules: the deadline is short). |
| **Consent for analytics** (s.6: free, specific, informed, unambiguous; withdrawal as easy as giving it) | Google Analytics loads only after the visitor clicks Accept in the cookie banner; Accept and Reject are equal buttons; nothing is pre-ticked; the site works the same after Reject. **Consent record:** the choice and its date are kept in the visitor's browser (`gb-consent`, 12 months, then asked again). **Withdrawal:** Cookie settings in every footer reopens the banner; Reject stops sending at once and deletes the `_ga` cookies. The Privacy Policy names consent (s.6, and GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) as the basis. | Keep the banner wording literally true (no advertising, nothing sold). If you ever use GA data for advertising or turn on Google signals, the notice and the consent must change first. |
| **Children** (s.9) | The policy says the site is for businesses, not intended for anyone under 18, and that their data is not knowingly collected. The site has no consumer features. | If an enquiry clearly comes from someone under 18, do not process it further and delete it (verifiable parental consent would otherwise be needed). |
| **Processors** (s.8(2): a valid contract with each Data Processor) | The policy names Google (Workspace, Sheets, Gmail), Google Analytics (Google LLC; Google Ireland Limited for EU and UK visitors) and Vercel Inc. Both process data under their standard terms: the Google Workspace terms with its Cloud Data Processing Addendum, and Vercel's terms with its Data Processing Addendum. WhatsApp (Meta) and Google Translate are used only when the visitor chooses them, under their own terms. | In GA4, accept the data processing terms offered under Admin → Account settings, and set data retention to 14 months. Keep the Google Workspace and Vercel accounts on business terms that include their data processing addenda (accept them in the admin consoles if asked). If an order shares details with a manufacturer, freight forwarder, customs broker, inspection agency or bank, share only what that order needs. |
| **Cross-border transfer** (s.16) | The policy says providers may store data outside India (for example the United States or the European Union), that Indian law allows this except to countries the Government restricts, and that EU and UK visitors are covered by recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, and that Google Analytics data is processed in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. | Check from time to time whether the Government has restricted transfers to any country your providers use. |

## Owner actions, in one list

- [ ] Turn on 2-step verification on the Google account (and on the Vercel account).
- [ ] Restrict the enquiry sheet's sharing to the people who answer enquiries.
- [ ] Calendar a deletion sweep for enquiries older than 24 months with no business.
- [ ] Keep a simple breach log.
- [ ] In GA4: data retention 14 months, Google signals off, data processing terms accepted (`docs/analytics.md`).
- [ ] Reply to privacy requests within 30 days (subject "Privacy request" to hygiene@goodbizglobal.com).

## Keep it true

The pages describe the site as built on 1 November 2026 (Google Analytics with consent added). **Before adding a chat widget, embedded maps or videos, a newsletter, or any other tool that stores or sends personal data,** update the Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy (and their 11 translations, `docs/i18n-translator-guide.md` section 3.8), change the "Last updated" date, and check whether it needs to go behind the cookie banner's consent too. A test (`tests/unit/legal.test.ts`) fails if the code uses a browser storage key the cookie page does not list.
