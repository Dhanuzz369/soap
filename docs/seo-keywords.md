# Keyword strategy: GoodBiz Hygiene

Last updated 2026-10-25. Built on buyer intent: importers, distributors, wholesalers and private-label brands looking for a supplier in India. Every phrase is one a buyer types; none is a promise we cannot keep.

## How the keywords are used

- **Primary keyword** of a product = its `keyword` field (src/content/products/<slug>.md and each translation). It drives the meta keywords and appears naturally in the page title, H1 (product name + "Exporter & Supplier from India"), the opening answer, the specification H2 ("What are the typical specifications of <product>?") and the photo alt text.
- **Meta keywords** (`<meta name="keywords">`, at most 12 per page) are generated from templates in each language's UI dictionary (`ui.seo.keywords` in src/i18n/ui/<lang>.ts), filled with the product keyword and HSN code. Google ignores this tag; Bing, Yandex, Baidu and Naver may read it. It is never shown to readers, so it cannot read as stuffing.
- **Titles and descriptions** carry the primary keyword and the buyer's value: private label, mixed containers, FOB/CIF. Titles stay within 60 characters before " | GoodBiz Hygiene" (32 for Chinese); descriptions 120-160 characters (72-100 for Chinese).
- **"Manufacturers India" searches** are targeted honestly: titles say "Exporter & Supplier", and the copy says GoodBiz Hygiene *sources from vetted Indian manufacturers*. That sentence matches the query without claiming to manufacture (spec §4 and §13).
- **Regional variants** ("<product> supplier UAE / Kenya / Nigeria / Saudi Arabia / Brazil") follow each product's listed markets. In other languages the two market phrases name the markets where that language is spoken (`productMarkets`).

Do not paste these lists into visible copy. Write for the buyer; use one or two phrases where they fit a sentence naturally.

## Maintaining the keywords

- New product: give it a `keyword` (what buyers type, lower case unless it is a name like LABSA) in the English file and in every translation; the meta keywords, alt text and headings follow automatically.
- Check real demand before changing a keyword: Google Search Console (Performance > Queries) after launch, Bing Webmaster Tools keyword research, Google Keyword Planner, and Yandex Wordstat / Baidu Index for Russian and Chinese.
- Re-check every quarter: keep phrases that bring impressions and enquiries, replace ones that bring none. Never add a term the business cannot honestly serve (for example "manufacturer" as a claim, prices, certifications).
