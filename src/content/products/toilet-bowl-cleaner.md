## Product overview

Toilet bowl cleaner is a core product in the household and facility cleaning range because limescale, rust rings and urine stains are a daily problem in regions with hard water. The product is designed to be thick enough to cling to the porcelain under the rim, giving the active ingredients time to loosen deposits before the toilet is brushed and flushed. Buyers compare brands on thickness, cleaning power, fragrance and the design of the angled bottle neck.

## Grades and fragrances

Standard grades are acid-based and typically sit at a very low pH, which gives strong action on scale and rust. Mild grades use gentler acids such as citric or sulphamic acid and suit buyers who want a lower-hazard product. Thickness is controlled with thickeners so the liquid clings, and a thick gel grade is available for premium positioning.

Fragrance covers pine, lemon and an ocean-fresh profile, and the colour is usually blue, green or red to help users see where the product has been placed. All typical figures for pH and viscosity are set out on the specification sheet, and a sample can be sent before you place a bulk order.

## Safety, labelling and shipping

Because toilet bowl cleaners are often acidic, the label needs hazard wording that suits the destination market, and shipping classification depends on strength and volume. The manufacturer provides a safety data sheet, and GoodBiz Hygiene confirms the transport classification per order before booking. Child-resistant caps can be fitted where required.
