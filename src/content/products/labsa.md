## Product overview

LABSA is the workhorse surfactant of the detergent industry. It is a sulphonated linear alkylbenzene that, once neutralised, becomes sodium linear alkylbenzene sulphonate, the active ingredient behind the foam and cleaning power in most washing powders, liquid detergents and household cleaners. Its low cost, strong cleaning performance and compatibility with builders make it the default anionic choice for high-volume detergent making.

We don't make LABSA. GoodBiz Hygiene sources it from vetted Indian chemical manufacturers, confirms the grade and packing against your formulation, and handles the export documents and shipping as your single contact.

## Grades and specifications

Two grades are common. LABSA 90% is the standard grade for detergent powder slurries and general cleaners. LABSA 96% has a higher active content and lower sulphuric acid and free oil, which helps in liquids where colour and clarity matter. Figures in the specification table are typical values, and the exact limits are confirmed on the specification sheet and certificate of analysis for your order.

## Packaging and shipping

LABSA is packed in 200 to 235 kg HDPE drums or 1,000 L IBCs, with ISO tanks on request for bulk loads. A 20 ft container typically holds around 16 to 18 tonnes in drums. Because the acid is corrosive, it moves as dangerous goods, with the correct UN marking and declarations. We coordinate this, and an MSDS is available on request.

## Handling and storage

LABSA is corrosive, so use acid-resistant equipment, gloves and eye protection. Storage tanks should be of suitable material, and the product should be kept closed and away from moisture. On neutralisation with caustic soda or soda ash, heat is released, so add slowly and mix well.
