## Grades and formulations

A dish wash powder combines surfactants with builders such as soda ash, sodium sulphate and fine mineral abrasives like calcium carbonate. General grades typically carry 8-15% active matter and suit price-led retail. Premium grades, typically 15-20%, give more foam and faster grease removal. An oxy-bleach version includes a peroxide-based booster that helps lift tea and coffee stains from cups and plastic containers.

Fragrance choices are lemon, lime and orange, with an unscented option for buyers who prefer a neutral product. Coloured speckles in green or yellow are often added so that the powder looks fresh and signals the scent on the pack. Particle size can be set finer for gentle use or coarser for a stronger scrubbing feel, so tell us which style sells in your market.
