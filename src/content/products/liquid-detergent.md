Liquid detergent has grown steadily wherever washing machines are spreading and shoppers are trading up from powder. It dissolves instantly in cold water, leaves no residue, pours cleanly and can be dosed more precisely. It also carries a higher price per litre, which makes it an attractive premium line for importers and retailers.

## Grades and formulations

A liquid detergent is built around a surfactant system, usually anionic and non-ionic actives, with water, builders, preservatives, thickener, colour and perfume. Economy liquids carry less active matter and are thinner. Premium liquids typically carry more actives, may include enzymes or optical brighteners, and have a richer viscosity.

Use also splits the range. Top-load liquids foam freely and work for hand washing too. Front-load liquids use a controlled-foam system. Delicates and woollens liquids are milder and often unperfumed or lightly perfumed, while colour-care liquids focus on protecting dyes. Popular fragrances include floral, lavender and fresh ocean, and colour is usually matched to the fragrance story.

## Packaging for export

Liquid detergent is sold in PET or HDPE bottles of 500 ml to 3 L, stand-up spout pouches for refills, and 5 L cans or 20 L jerrycans for bulk. Master cartons hold 6 to 20 units. Because the product is dense and heavy, container payload usually limits the load before volume does. A loading plan can be arranged for your chosen size mix.
