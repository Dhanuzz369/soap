## Product overview

Soap noodles are the starting material for most solid soap made around the world. Fats and oils are saponified, dried and extruded into noodles, which soap makers then mix with fragrance, colour, titanium dioxide and other additives, mill, plod and stamp into finished bars. Buying noodles lets a soap maker skip the capital-heavy saponification step and focus on finishing and branding.

GoodBiz Hygiene is a merchant exporter from India and a GoodBiz Global company. We do not make the noodles ourselves. We source them from vetted Indian manufacturers, confirm the grade against your requirement and manage documentation and shipping as a single point of contact.

## Grades and specifications

Quality is defined mainly by the fatty blend and Total Fatty Matter. The 80:20 palm to palm kernel (PKO) blend is the standard choice for toilet and bath soap, giving a firm bar with good wear. The 70:30 blend adds more palm kernel oil and gives quicker lather, which many buyers prefer for premium or fast-lathering positioning. Economy grades with lower TFM are used for laundry and utility bars where price matters more than skin feel.

Figures shown in the specification table are typical values and are confirmed on the technical data sheet for your order. A certificate of analysis (COA) per batch can be requested. Moisture is the key figure to watch, because it affects milling, bar hardness and weight.

## Packaging for export

Noodles ship in 25 kg and 50 kg woven bags with a moisture-barrier liner. Bags are palletised and stretch-wrapped for sea freight. A 20 ft container typically carries around 18 to 20 tonnes, and a 40 ft container around 24 to 26 tonnes, depending on bag size and palletising. Loading plans are shared with each quotation.

## Who buys soap noodles

Typical buyers are soap manufacturers who finish their own bars, contract soap packers, and distributors who supply many small factories in one region. If you are building a product range, soap noodles pair naturally with surfactants such as LABSA and SLES, and with finished bars for resale.
