## Product overview

SLES is one of the most widely used surfactants in personal care and home care. It lathers well, rinses clearly and is gentler on skin than many alternatives, which is why it forms the foaming base of shampoos, body washes, liquid hand washes and dish wash liquids. In heavier-duty products it works alongside LABSA to build cleaning power and foam.

We don't make SLES. GoodBiz Hygiene sources it from vetted Indian surfactant manufacturers, confirms the grade against your formulation, and handles the documents and the shipping as your single contact.

## Grades and specifications

SLES is sold mainly at 28% and 70% active. The 28% liquid is simple to use and is the common choice for small and mid-size blenders. The 70% paste is more concentrated and suits buyers with heating and mixing facilities who want to cut freight per tonne of active. Ethoxylation level, usually 1, 2 or 3 moles, affects mildness and viscosity response with salt, and can be specified. Figures in the table are typical values, confirmed on the specification sheet and certificate of analysis for each order.

## Packaging for export

SLES comes in 200 to 235 kg HDPE drums, 1,000 L IBCs, or flexitanks of about 20 tonnes. The 70% paste is thick and needs warming to pump or pour, so packing and unloading plans should allow for this. A 20 ft container holds roughly 16 to 18 tonnes in drums. Classification for transport is confirmed per order.

## Formulation notes

SLES thickens with salt, so many formulators build viscosity with sodium chloride. pH is usually adjusted to 5.5 to 7 for personal care and left higher for cleaners. Pair with a co-surfactant such as cocamidopropyl betaine for added mildness and foam stability.
