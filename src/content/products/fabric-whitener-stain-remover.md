## Product overview

Even a good detergent leaves yellowing on white cottons and stubborn marks on collars, cuffs and kitchen linen. Fabric whiteners and stain removers solve those problems, either as a boost during the wash or as a pre-treatment. They are inexpensive add-ons that shoppers buy repeatedly, and they sit well alongside detergent and softener on the same shelf.

## Formats and formulations

Laundry blue is the traditional whitener, a liquid that adds a faint blue tint to cancel yellowing and is still popular in many markets. Optical brightener products work differently, by converting UV light into visible blue light. Oxygen-based stain removers use a percarbonate source that releases active oxygen in water, lifting stains without the harshness of chlorine bleach. These come as powders, liquids and, for pre-treatment, gels or sprays.

Powder boosters are the cheapest to ship and store, and can be sold in sachets. Liquids are easier to dose and spot-treat. Pre-wash gels target heavily soiled spots before the main wash. Fragrance is optional, and many oxygen products are sold unperfumed to emphasise their cleaning role.

## Packaging for export

This category spans powder boosters, liquids and pre-wash gels, so the packing differs by format. Powders go in laminated pouches or tubs of 400 g to 1 kg, liquids in bottles with a pour or spray cap, and bulk liquid in 5 L cans or 20 L jerrycans. Master cartons hold 12 to 24 retail units. Oxygen-active powders must be kept dry in transit.
