## Product overview

The dish wash bar is a staple in many markets where shoppers buy by the unit and every cent counts. A bar is easy to store and handle, and a single cake can last for several weeks of daily use. Compared with a bottle of liquid, it uses less packaging and lighter freight, which suits distributors who sell into rural areas or smaller shops.

GoodBiz Hygiene doesn't run detergent lines. We source dish wash bars from vetted Indian bar-soap makers, check each order against your specification, and handle the export documents and shipping as your single contact.

## Grades and formulations

Dish wash bars are made from a blend of surfactants, mild alkali, fillers and a binder that holds the bar together while it sits in water. Standard bars typically have 35-45% active matter and deliver good grease removal and a stable lather. Economy bars with typically 20-30% active matter lower the shelf price and still handle everyday dishes. The best bars stay firm in a wet soap dish and do not turn to slime.

Lemon and lime are the best sellers, usually in a green or yellow colour that tells shoppers what they are buying. Orange offers a variation, and an antibacterial lemon version suits buyers who want that positioning, subject to local rules. An unscented bar is available for buyers who prefer a neutral product. Some bars include scrubbing fibres inside, while tub packs include a separate scrub pad.

## Packaging for export

Dish wash bars come as a flow-wrapped bar, a bar with a scouring pad, or a bar in a lidded plastic tub of 200 g to 300 g. Multipacks of three or four bars suit supermarkets, and master cartons hold 48 to 144 bars. The bars are heavy, so payload limits a container, and a loading plan can be arranged for your pack type.

## Private label options

Brands choose between bar only, bar with scrub pad and tub pack, each at a different price point. Colour and scent, usually lemon, lime or green lime, come from the manufacturer's range, and bar weights follow its moulds. Tub artwork needs a carton proof and a sample before production. Lead time is typically 3-5 weeks after order confirmation.
