## Product overview

Body wash and shower gel have taken a large share of the personal wash market from bar soap, particularly in urban households, hotels and fitness centres. Consumers like the rich lather, the wide choice of fragrances and the hygienic pump dispenser. For importers, the category offers a good margin on private label and a strong link to hospitality supply, where the same fragrance can be sold in retail bottles and hotel refill formats.

## Grades and formulations

Body wash is built on mild surfactants with active matter typically in the 10-16% range, balanced to a skin-friendly pH of about 5.5-7.0 and thickened to a gel. Entry grades are clear and fresh, while premium grades are pearlescent and add conditioners such as glycerin, aloe or almond milk. Viscosity usually falls in the 3,000-8,000 cP range.

Fragrance sets the positioning. Ocean fresh and citrus appeal to a younger sport audience, lavender and chamomile suit a relaxing range, sandalwood and saffron fit a traditional Indian-inspired line, charcoal speaks to detox, and rose with almond milk suits a feminine premium line.
