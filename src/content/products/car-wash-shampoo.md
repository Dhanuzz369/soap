## Product overview

Car wash shampoo is an automotive aftermarket product that sits comfortably beside household cleaners in an importer's range. Drivers want a thick, lubricating foam that lifts road film and dust without scratching the paint or stripping the wax. Because the product is sold as a concentrate, one bottle washes many vehicles, so buyers compare brands on foam, rinsing, fragrance and how far the product dilutes.

## Types and formulations

Plain concentrate is the entry-level product and is preferred by garages and car wash centres that buy in 5 L and 20 L cans. Wash and wax grades add a polymer or wax component for extra gloss and suit retail shelves. Foam lance or snow foam grades are tuned for pressure-washer equipment and give a thicker, clinging foam. Fragrance options include strawberry, cherry and a fresh or ocean profile, and colour is usually pink, red or blue.

Typical pH, active matter and dilution ratios are shared on the specification sheet. Because many buyers wash vehicles with hard water, a sample is often tested on a local car before the first order.
