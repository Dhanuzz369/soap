## How to choose laundry products for your market

Most markets buy laundry products in more than one format. Laundry soap bars (HSN 3401 19) remain the everyday choice where clothes are scrubbed by hand. Detergent powder (3402 50) has a long history in bucket and basin washing and gives the most entry-level price points, while liquid detergent (3402 50) is gaining ground in machine washing and is usually positioned as the convenient, premium choice. Our comparison of [detergent powder and liquid detergent](/blog/detergent-powder-vs-liquid-detergent/) sets out the deciding factors, from washing habits to freight cost per wash.

Fabric softener completes the shelf. It is usually classified under 3402 50 but sometimes under 3809 91, so confirm the code with your broker, and see the [fabric softener buying guide](/blog/fabric-softener-buying-guide-importers/) for types and fragrances. Sacks, cartons and bottles from the range can share one 20 ft or 40 ft container, so a distributor can launch a full laundry shelf in one shipment.
