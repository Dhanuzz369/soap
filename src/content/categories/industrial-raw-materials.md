## How to choose industrial and raw materials

This range is for buyers who make or blend their own products. Soap noodles (HSN 3401 20) are the base for bar soap and are bought by blend and TFM. LABSA (3402 31) and SLES (3402 39) are the main surfactants for detergents, dish wash and hand wash, and they sit in different HS 2022 subheadings from finished cleaners. Bulk institutional cleaners (3402 90) are concentrates for hotels, facility-service companies and distributors who decant them locally. Our guide to [HSN codes 3401 and 3402](/blog/hsn-code-3401-vs-3402-soaps-detergents/) explains why each product sits where it does.

These goods ship in bags, drums, IBCs or flexitanks rather than retail packs. LABSA is corrosive and moves as dangerous goods, and strongly acidic or alkaline concentrates may too, so read the [export documents guide](/blog/export-documents-cleaning-products/) before you book freight. Specifications such as active matter and TFM are typical values, confirmed with the manufacturer for each order, and these products are supplied without private label.
