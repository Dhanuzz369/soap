## How to choose bath and personal soaps

Bars and liquids answer different shelves. Toilet, herbal, glycerin, antibacterial and hotel bars are all classified as toilet soap under HSN 3401 11 and are compared mainly on TFM, bar weight and fragrance; our guide to [bath soap grades and TFM](/blog/bath-soap-grades-tfm-explained/) explains how to read a grade before you compare prices. Liquid hand wash and body wash sit under 3401 30, so expect a second code on the paperwork when you order bars and liquids together.

Mixing bars and liquids in one 20 ft or 40 ft container lets you trial both formats in a single shipment; the mix is confirmed against packing and compatibility rules when we quote. Hotels and resorts often combine wrapped guest soap with hand wash and shower gel in matching fragrances. Private label is available across the range: send your artwork, preferred fragrances and pack sizes, and each line is quoted per pack.
