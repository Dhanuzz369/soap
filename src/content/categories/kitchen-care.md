## How to choose kitchen care products

Kitchen care comes in two formats. Dish wash liquid (HSN 3402 50) is sold in bottles for households and in cans and drums for restaurants and caterers. Dish wash bars and tubs (3401 19) remain popular where dishes are scrubbed by hand with a pad, and they are classified as soap, not as detergent. Many importers start with one liquid and one bar in the fragrances their market already knows, such as lemon and lime, then add pack sizes once the first order sells.

Both formats can share a 20 ft or 40 ft container with products from the other ranges, and the mix is confirmed when we quote.
