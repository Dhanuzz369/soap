## Analytics cookies, only if you accept

This website uses cookies only for Google Analytics, and only if you accept them in the cookie banner. They show us which pages and products buyers look at, so that we can improve the website. Until you accept, the Google Analytics code is not loaded at all and no cookies are set. We use no advertising or social media trackers, and no embedded content from other websites, such as videos or maps. Fonts and images are served from our own website.

## What we store in your browser

A few features keep information in your browser's own storage. It stays on your device: nothing is sent to us or anyone else unless you send an enquiry. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics also sets the two cookies in the last rows of the table on our domain, and your browser sends them to Google Analytics with each page you visit.

| What it remembers | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The language you picked in the language menu (`gb-lang`). Saved only when you pick one. | So the website does not switch you to another language on your next visit. | Until you clear it in your browser. |
| The products you tick and the type of business you choose on the HoReCa page list (`gb-horeca`). No personal data. Saved as soon as you open that page, empty until you tick something. | So your list is still there when you come back to the HoReCa page. | Until you clear it in your browser. A new number is made after you send an enquiry, or when you open the form from a link with different products. |
| Your answers on the first step of the enquiry form: name, company, email and phone (`goodbiz-enquiry-contact`). Saved when you tap Next. | So a reload of the page keeps them and you do not type them again. | Until you close the tab. Deleted after your enquiry is sent successfully. |

## Your choice, and changing your mind

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept Google Analytics cookies. Accept and Reject are equal choices, and rejecting changes nothing else on the website: every page and the enquiry form work the same. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the foot of every page. If you withdraw your consent, Google Analytics stops at once and we delete its cookies on our domain. We ask again after 12 months. Google Analytics is set up without advertising features: Google signals and ad personalisation are off, and nothing about you is sold. What Google Analytics receives, and how long it is kept, is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Clearing it

You can delete this information at any time in your browser settings ("clear site data"). The HoReCa list also has a Clear button. Clearing site data also removes your cookie choice, so the banner asks again.

## Links to other websites

- Links to WhatsApp, Google Translate and government or registration websites take you to sites with their own privacy and cookie policies.
- Nothing is sent to those sites until you click.
