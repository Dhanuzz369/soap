## Analytics cookies, and where we ask first

This website uses cookies only for Google Analytics. They show us which pages and products buyers look at, so that we can improve the website. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in the countries where one of this website's other languages is spoken (for example the Arab countries, French-, Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking countries, Russia, China, Turkey, Indonesia, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and Vietnam), we ask first: until you accept, the Google Analytics code is not loaded at all and no cookies are set. In other countries, such as India and the USA, Google Analytics runs by default, and you can switch it off at any time. We tell where you are from your browser's time zone, and Google also checks your approximate location, so a visitor from a country where we ask first is not measured without a choice. We use no advertising or social media trackers, and no embedded content from other websites, such as videos or maps. Fonts and images are served from our own website.

## What we store in your browser

A few features keep information in your browser's own storage. It stays on your device: nothing is sent to us or anyone else unless you send an enquiry. While Google Analytics runs (after you accept, or by default where we do not ask first), it also sets the two cookies in the last rows of the table on our domain, and your browser sends them to Google Analytics with each page you visit.

## Your choice, and changing your mind

Where we ask first, a banner asks on your first visit whether you accept Google Analytics cookies, and asks again after 12 months. Accept and Reject are equal choices, and rejecting changes nothing else on the website: every page and the enquiry form work the same. Where Google Analytics runs by default, no banner appears. Everywhere, you can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the foot of every page. If you reject, Google Analytics stops at once and we delete its cookies on our domain. Google Analytics is set up without advertising features: Google signals and ad personalisation are off, and nothing about you is sold. What Google Analytics receives, and how long it is kept, is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Clearing it

You can delete this information at any time in your browser settings ("clear site data"). The HoReCa list also has a Clear button. Clearing site data also removes your cookie choice, so the banner asks again.

## Links to other websites

- Links to WhatsApp, Google Translate and government or registration websites take you to sites with their own privacy and cookie policies.
- Nothing is sent to those sites until you click.
