## No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies. It has no analytics, advertising or social media trackers, and no embedded content from other websites, such as videos or maps. Fonts and images are served from our own website.

## What we store in your browser

A few features keep information in your browser's own storage. It stays on your device: nothing is sent to us or anyone else unless you send an enquiry.

| What it remembers | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The language you picked in the language menu (`gb-lang`). Saved only when you pick one. | So the website does not switch you to another language on your next visit. | Until you clear it in your browser. |
| The products you tick and the type of business you choose on the HoReCa page list (`gb-horeca`). No personal data. Saved as soon as you open that page, empty until you tick something. | So your list is still there when you come back to the HoReCa page. | Until you clear it in your browser. The list's Clear button empties the products. |
| A random enquiry number (`goodbiz-enquiry-lead`). Made when you open the contact page. | So the steps of one enquiry, and a reload of the page, stay together as one enquiry. | Until you close the tab. A new number is made after you send an enquiry, or when you open the form from a link with different products. |
| Your answers on the first step of the enquiry form: name, company, email and phone (`goodbiz-enquiry-contact`). Saved when you tap Next. | So a reload of the page keeps them and you do not type them again. | Until you close the tab. Deleted after your enquiry is sent successfully. |
| The products the enquiry started with, when you opened the form from a product link (`goodbiz-enquiry-products`). | So a link with different products starts a new enquiry instead of changing the last one. | Until you close the tab. |

## Why there is no cookie banner

We store only what is strictly necessary for features you choose to use, and nothing is used to track you, so we do not need to ask for consent. If we ever add analytics or other tools that need your consent, we will update this page and ask you first.

## Clearing it

You can delete this information at any time in your browser settings ("clear site data"). The HoReCa list also has a Clear button.

## Links to other websites

- Links to WhatsApp, Google Translate and government or registration websites take you to sites with their own privacy and cookie policies.
- Nothing is sent to those sites until you click.
